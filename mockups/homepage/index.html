Why we exist

Every child deserves to keep their spark.

Difficult family, economic and social circumstances can wear down a child’s confidence, enthusiasm and hope for the future. We exist so that children rediscover joy and believe that their circumstances do not have to define their future.

  • Potential over pity
  • Hope over helplessness
  • Dignity over dependency
  • Empowerment over limitation
About us

Cheer d’Kids is an NGO working across Southwest Nigeria, with documented outreaches in Lagos and Kwara.

Our mission

To provide programs and activities that enhance happiness and instill self-belief in children.

Our vision

To ensure that every child experiences joy and has the hope to believe in their dreams and aspirations.
